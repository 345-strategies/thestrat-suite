v0.3.3
[list]
[*][b]Ladder Size:[/b] the cascade ladder letters (¼, ½, H, D, W, M) can now be Tiny, Small, Normal, Large or Huge. The gap between stacked letters grows with the size, and a Spacing setting fine-tunes it.
[*][b]Alerts:[/b] get an alert when a timeframe goes in force, the same events the ladder marks. Pick timeframes and Bull / Bear in the new Alerts group, then Create Alert > this study > "Any alert() function call". Optional alert when FTFC and every timeframe in force agree. Fires on bar close by default.
[/list]

v0.3.2
[list]
[*][b]Outside bars count as in force:[/b] a 3 closing above C1's high is in force bull, below C1's low in force bear. Closing back inside C1 is not in force.
[*][b]Score split:[/b] bull and bear counts plotted separately, so they no longer cancel out.
[*][b]Shared opens:[/b] the heatmap fades a timeframe's open when it shares it with a lower timeframe (Monday W = D, first trading day M = D, first hour D = H).
[*][b]Optional 15m and 30m timeframes[/b], off by default.
[*]Cascade ladder now drawn as labels (most recent 500) to stay under the plot limit.
[/list]
